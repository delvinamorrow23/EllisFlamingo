The short version

Kindergarten runs on an 8-day rotation instead of a Monday-to-Friday week. Each regular school day gets the next number: Day 1, Day 2, and so on up to Day 8, then back to Day 1.

  • Weekends and no-school days don't count. The rotation picks up where it left off on the next school day.
  • LAB days aren't counted in the 1–8 rotation, but the teachers can run one as a specific day. Example: Tue Sep 15 was Day 2. Wed Sep 16 was a LAB day run as Day 1, to make up for the half-day first day. Thu Sep 17 is Day 3.
  • Watch the Friday Seesaw newsletter. Its "Days" box lists the coming week, including which schedule a LAB day follows.
  • PE uniform is worn on Days 1, 5, and 6. The teachers confirmed this, and it matches the days PE is on the schedule.
  • Day 1 was the first day of school, Tue Sep 1 (half day, 12:45 dismissal).

The other LAB days this year are Oct 7, Oct 28, Nov 18, Dec 16, Jan 27, Feb 24, and Apr 28. This app will say which day each one follows once the teachers announce it.

Worth confirming with the teachers

This app copies the day numbers printed on the school's draft calendar (dated 6/17/2026). A few spots on that draft don't follow the pattern above:

Oct 26 is printed as Day 2.Oct 21 is Day 8, and Oct 22–23 are conference days, so you'd expect Oct 26 to be Day 1.
Apr 26 is printed as Day 7.Apr 21 is Day 4, and Apr 22–23 are conference days, so you'd expect Day 5.
Mar 17–19 have no day numbers, and Apr 5 is printed as Day 8.Mar 16 is Day 5, so you'd expect Apr 5 to be Day 6. That suggests Days 6 and 7 happen during the musical days. Also, the calendar key says "March 18–20," but the shaded dates are the 17th through 19th.
Dec 18 and Jun 4 have no day numbers.Dec 18 is a half day, and Jun 4 is the last day of school.
Apr 9 (Day 4) is shaded gray on the printout, and the key doesn't say what gray means.
Lunch time.The schedule sheet's time labels are slightly out of line. This app shows lunch at 11:00–11:25.
